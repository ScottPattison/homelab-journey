Why I'm Doing This
After my third kidney transplant, and with my previous employer going out of business, I found myself at a bit of a crossroads. I've always been interested in tech, so I decided to give it a proper go and see if I could turn that interest into an actual career.
I do genuinely enjoy this stuff - I've already got an AAT bookkeeping qualification and a Level 2 in Cyber Security, and I've taught myself bits of AI and LLMs along the way. It feels like a good time to bring all of that together and put it to use.
Misha's Free Linux Course felt like the right starting point, and the natural next step from there would be joining the Kubecraft community - but the cost makes that a non-starter for now, so I'm focusing on what's free and accessible.
I'd be lying if I said I wasn't worried about a few things. My age is one - breaking into tech in your 50s isn't exactly the norm. And my health is another - I feel like I have to be upfront about my history at interview stage, which might be as far as things go for some employers.
But I'm doing this anyway, because what's the alternative? Might as well try.
