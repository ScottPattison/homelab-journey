# About Me

In my late 20's my kidney failed for the first time, I was on CAPD dialysis for a year before my first transplant.  The transplant failed 4 years later and I spent the next 2 years on Haemodialysis before my next transplant. While all of that was going on I managed to get a BTEC in computing (early 90's), and I am a qualified AAT book keeper. I am also into anything tech, having taught myself in any spare time I had available, gaining a level 2 certification in Cyber Security in the process, I worked as a store manager for the retail firm Wilko, until I had to leave due to my dialysis restrictions.
