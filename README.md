Journaling my descent down the homelabbing, Linux and networking rabbit hole.

# About Me!
Hi, I'm Scott - 52, based in Andover, Hampshire and making a somewhat overdue career change into tech.

My health history is, to put it mildly, evenful. Brain abcess at 15, epilepsy from the scarring, two kidney transplants that didn't quite get the memo about lasting forever, and the best part of a decade on dialysis across two seperate stints.  Third transplant is now onboard and finally behaving itself - touch wood, cross fingers, sacrifice a goat, the usual.  My body has essentially been a long running IT project with poor change management and several unexpected roll-backs.

Despite all that - or possibly because of that - I managed to pick up a BTEC in Computing (early 90's), qualify as an AAT book keeper, and taught myself everything tech-related I could squeeze into whatever spare time dialysis left me with.  That included a level 2 Cyber Security certification, which at least proves I can pass an exam when I'm not attached to a machine for four hours a day, 3 days a week. I spent years managing Wilko stores, until the company went into administration in 2023 and took my job with it.

So - recovering from a 3rd transplant, unexpectedly unemployeed, and with more time on my hands than i've had in years. Seemed like the perfect moment to finally do the thing i'd always wanted to do and properly commit to a career in tech. Some people take up gardening, I build a homelab.
